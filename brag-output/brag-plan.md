# /brag plan: North Hills recruiting site

**Input:** joincbnorthhills.com (site source in this repo, rendered live in headless Chromium)
**Route:** /brag-slim (Hyperframes skills not installed in this environment)
**Tone:** polished (serious, elegant, restrained, long holds, soft fades)
**Format:** landscape 1920x1080, 30fps, about 21s

## Rubric

- **What is it:** a private recruiting page where Dana Coon, Sales Director at Coldwell Banker Realty North Hills, invites agents to a confidential conversation.
- **Who is it for:** experienced agents thinking about moving their business, and newer agents who need a first year that works.
- **What sets it apart:** it talks to agents as businesses, not headcount. First meeting is confidential. The office has its own Agent Hub.
- **Most impressive claim:** "You're not a seat to fill. You're a business to grow."
- **Visual hook:** the lit-up North Hills office at dusk, headline over it.
- **Real UI shown:** the two "ways in" cards, the six HOME platform cards, the Agent Hub window (Calendar, Floor Time, Open Houses). All captured from the live page.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt

## Angle

Say the thesis first (the hook is the site's own headline), open the door, prove it with real product, close on a confidential, low-pressure ask.

## Identity

Colors: canvas #00123d, panel #0a1d52, accent #1f69ff, text #eef2fb, secondary #a9b8d9, paper #f3f5f9, ink #0b1430.
Fonts: Jost (headlines, body), IBM Plex Mono (eyebrows). Same as the site.

## Sound

Soft pad in D major (Dmaj7, Bm7, Gmaj7, Asus4/A), slow arpeggio from the reveal, small bell ticks on card entrances, quiet cursor clicks, short air swells on scene changes. Everything shares the key and one reverb. No voiceover.

## Guardrails

No invented claims, numbers or testimonials. All on-screen copy is lifted from the site. The Hub window is labeled on the site as an illustration of the real tool, so the video frames it as "a hub built just for this office" and does not claim live data.
